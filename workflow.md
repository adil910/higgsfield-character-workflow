# Workflow

## A. Recurring character

### 1. Lock the character sheet
Write one fixed description and reuse it word for word in every prompt:

> A tall man, about 6 ft, athletic build, wearing a matte black full-face mask with a small gold "A" on the forehead, an all-black tailored suit, black shirt, black gloves and black leather shoes. No skin visible.

### 2. Generate the reference set (about 20 images)
- Front, three-quarter left, three-quarter right, profile, back
- Standing, walking, sitting, stepping out of a car
- Close-up of mask, close-up of gloves
- Same neutral lighting for most shots, plus a few golden hour and night shots

### 3. Reject anything inconsistent
Throw out images where the mask shape, the "A", suit cut or height changes. Twenty consistent images beat forty mixed ones.

### 4. Create the Element
Upload the approved set, name it clearly (e.g. "Ace masked") and test with three simple prompts before using it in a real shot.

## B. Car Element

1. Collect 25–35 real photos of the same car: all angles, close-ups of wheels, lights and badges.
2. Remove photos with heavy reflections of people, strong filters or other cars in frame.
3. Create the Element and test front, side and rear generations.
4. Check wheels, light signatures and badges every time. These drift first.

## C. Shot prompt template

> [Element name] [action], [location], [time of day / lighting], [camera angle and lens], [camera movement]. Keep [character/car] consistent with the reference. Photorealistic, cinematic grade, no text.

## D. Consistency checklist (every shot)

- Same mask, logo and outfit details (gloves present)
- Same height and build relative to the car
- Car shape, wheels and lights match the real car
- Lighting matches between cuts in the same scene
- No extra fingers, melted edges or warped text
